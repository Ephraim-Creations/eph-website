01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I’m Ephraim.

I’m the founder of Ephraim Creations, where I’m focused on making it easier for businesses to show up and sell online.

An online store is more than a list of products. It needs to make browsing feel simple for customers and keep the work behind every order manageable for the people running it. That’s the thinking behind the e-commerce platform I’m building.

I care about useful technology: clear storefronts, smoother order handling, and tools that make sense in the real rhythm of a business. The goal is straightforward: give good products a better way to reach people.
